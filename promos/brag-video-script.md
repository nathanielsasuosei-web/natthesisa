# CodeMaster Ghana — brag video script & storyboard

**Site:** codemasterghana — https://www.codemastergh.online
**Tagline:** Learn. Build. Become.
**Target length:** ~60 seconds (vertical 9:16 master, horizontal 16:9 crop)
**Brand:** violet `#6d4aff` / `#5c3be4`, sunshine `#ffcf59`, mint `#b8f0dc`, ink `#15131b`, paper `#fdfdfb`
**Voice:** warm, confident, Ghanaian-English. On-screen captions in ALL CAPS Montserrat-style.

All facts below are pulled from the live codebase (28 courses / 12 programs / 148 lessons, verified against `src/lib/courses.ts` + `src/lib/course-content.ts`).

## Production notes
- **Format:** vertical 1080×1920 master (TikTok / Reels / WhatsApp status), high-energy hype cut, ~60s.
- **Pricing is deliberately out** — the video ends on value + the free-account CTA instead.
- Narration: `voice-00` (English), one TTS clip per scene, assembled with ffmpeg; Ken Burns drift on stills, captions burned in, subtle synth beat bed.
- Intro/outro carry the real logo (`public/branding/logo-1024.jpg`).
- Export: `promos/codemasterghana-brag-9x16.mp4`. A 16:9 crop can be rendered on request.
